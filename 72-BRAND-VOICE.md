# 72 — Brand Voice & Messaging

The source of truth for how 72 talks. When writing any public copy, headline, or
pitch, start here. (Forged with Robert, 2026-07 — his voice, his metaphor.)

## The core metaphor: the table
72 is **a seat at your table.** You're the host. People *earn* the seat by proving
they're serious. This is the whole identity — not a phone app, not "pay to talk."

## The one move that makes it work: the status flip
"Pay to talk to me" sounds needy → people scoff.
"You decide who sits at your table" sounds exclusive → people lean in.
Same mechanic, opposite gravity. **Every piece of copy must keep the flip:** the
creator is the host holding the table, never the beggar asking to be paid.

## The lines (locked)

**Brand equation** (72 = the access itself, not just a percentage):
> **72 is the seat at the table.**

**One-liner** (the scoff-killer — drop this on a skeptic):
> A seat at your table isn't free — the ones who mean it prove it. That's 72.

**Manifesto** (the "why" — for the site's about/hero support):
> Harassers, scammers, posers, and time-wasters have no place at your table.
> Anyone vying for a seat proves it. Paid access to your time. **That's 72.**

## The engine (use this to generate new copy in-voice)
Every winning line hits three beats, in this order:
1. **Status flip** — you're the host / it's your table (kills the scoff)
2. **The filter** — only serious people get a seat (this is the *benefit* people feel)
3. **The proof** — you keep 72% (the legitimacy stamp, comes last, not first)

End cold on **72**. The stamp should be a full stop, not a soft sell: "…That's 72."

## Words to avoid
- **"commodity"** — economically means cheap/interchangeable, the opposite of the
  scarce, unrepeatable thing (your time) we're describing. Use "your time" / "the
  one thing you can't make more of."
- **"placer"** — reads as gold-mining or Spanish. The concept (the thing that seats
  you) is right; the word isn't. Use "the seat," "the ante," or "the cover."
- Leading with the **phone number / the 72% / the mechanics.** Nobody feels plumbing.
  Lead with the feeling (the table, the filter); the number is proof, not hook.

## Note for site copy
Legitimacy is **priced** (72% to the creator) but must also be **said** — the customer
doesn't feel the fairness automatically. Say the story out loud, or it's just a number.
